HARZ OFFLINE SHELL

WhatsApp Web works through a live encrypted link between this browser and your phone. That link needs network, so chats cannot load offline — on any browser, by design.

What still works offline right now: your recent chats stay readable in your phone's WhatsApp app, and your HARZ browser itself keeps working with everything stored in it.

When network returns, open web.whatsapp.com and the live service resumes. This shell exists so HARZ Browser never shows you a dead page.
