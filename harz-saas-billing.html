Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I hit my limit?
API calls return a 429 status. Upgrade your plan or wait for next month's reset. You'll get email alerts at 80% and 100% usage.
Can I switch plans mid-month?
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
Do you offer annual billing?
Yes — annual plans get 2 months free (pay 10 months, get 12). Select yearly when checking out.
What payment methods are accepted?
UBA bank transfer, Paystack (cards), GDEG token (10% discount), and USDT TRC20. All processed through HarzPay.
